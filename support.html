How to play

Cubes roll toward you across the grid. Stand on a tile in front of a grey or green cube and tap MARK. When the cube rolls onto the mark, tap CAPTURE. Green cubes leave a blast marker: tap BLAST to clear everything around it. Let red cubes roll away – destroying one costs you. Every wanted cube that rolls off the edge costs a row of floor.

Modes

Campaign: conquer 9 worlds. Endless: random waves that never stop. Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Unstable for either.
