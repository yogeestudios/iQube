iQube: Intelligent Blocks – Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

iQube: Intelligent Blocks (“the game”) is developed by Gary Gallagher (Oozies). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, settings, scores, coins, gems, upgrades and purchase status are saved only on your device. We do not operate servers and do not collect, sell or share this information. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version of the game shows ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To show and measure ads, Unity Ads may collect your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address and approximate location, device and operating-system information, and information about how you interact with ads.

The first time you play, iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you allow it, ads may be personalised. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Purchasing Remove Ads turns off banner and between-game ads. Optional reward videos you choose to watch remain available.

Unity’s privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

In-app purchases

All purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells the game only that a purchase was completed, so it can unlock what you bought.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple’s Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see them alongside your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings at any time, and you can delete all game data by deleting the game. For questions or requests about your data, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
